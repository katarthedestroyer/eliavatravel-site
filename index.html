What goes out, and when

  1. 120 days Deposit reminder Sent. Deposit paid on time.
  2. 90 days Final payment Reminder sent 14 days before. Paid.
  3. 60 days Supplier check Confirmations match the itinerary.
  4. 30 days Final itinerary Built and checked before it reaches you.
  5. 7 days Final documents Sent. You approved the email.
  6. Wheels up Welcome-home email Set for 2 days after they land.
