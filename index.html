Every step, and when

  1. 300 days New inquiry Answered the same day. Your call is booked.
  2. 290 days Proposal Built in your tool, ready to send.
  3. 270 days Deposit day Client profiles, invoice and agreement set up that day.
  4. Weekly Quiet months Payments, passports and supplier documents checked.
  5. 90 days Final payment Reminder sent 14 days before. Paid.
  6. 60 days Supplier check Hotels reconfirmed. Everything matches the itinerary.

  9. Wheels up On the trip Inbox watched. Anything urgent goes to you right away.
  10. Home Welcome-home email Sent 2 days after they land.
  11. After Follow-up Commission tracked. Review requested. Notes saved for next time.
