How it works

One kind of trip at a time.

We start with one kind of trip, like cruises. The next kind goes faster and costs less.

  1. 01

    We look at one trip together

    I show you what goes out, when, and what to fix.

  2. 02

    I set it up for you

    Emails, reminders and checklists for that kind of trip, inside your system. I write your emails from emails you've already sent, so they sound like you.

  3. 03

    You stay in charge

    You approve every email. You get a short guide with screenshots. It shows what is set up and how to change it.

  4. 04

    No explaining the travel side

    I already know hotel perks, cruise payments, DMCs and commission.

About me

Hi, I'm Kady!

My background is in operations long before travel. I spent years coordinating large-scale training events and executive travel, then brought that same discipline into luxury travel agencies.

I build the setup so the details don't depend on your memory, and I get the small things right: the welcome email that sounds like you wrote it, the wheelchair request that makes it to the hotel. That matters with every client, and most with the ones who need extra care: first-time travelers, older clients, and travelers with accessibility needs.

When I'm not working, I'm on my acreage in rural Georgia with my dog and kids, usually deep in a botany or mushroom rabbit hole, or planning the next trip.
