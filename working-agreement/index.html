01

Why this page exists.

Many advisors have tried help before and ended up redoing the work, or got a system nobody could explain. This page is how we avoid that.

It sets out what we promise, what helps the work go well, and what slows it down. Everything we ask of you, we hold ourselves to first.

03

Keeping in touch.

Work happens by email, on your schedule. You get updates in writing, so you can read them between client calls.

What works well

  • You read updates when it suits you, and reply within one or two business days.
  • Questions come in one email, not ten.
  • A call is booked only when talking will truly save time.

What slows us down

  • A call to cover what an email can.
  • Notes spread across texts, messages and email.

04

Your voice.

Every client email is written from ones you've already sent. Nothing goes out until you say yes.

What works well

  • You send a few emails you're proud of.
  • You point out words you'd never use.
  • You tell us which clients like it formal and which like it warm.

What slows us down

  • "Just write something," with no examples.
  • Rewriting a draft from scratch instead of telling us what's off.

05

Approvals.

You approve every email first. Approvals are where the work speeds up or stalls.

What works well

  • One person gives the final yes.
  • Notes come with a reason: "too stiff," or "this client hates exclamation points."

What slows us down

  • Three people sending different notes on the same email.
  • Reopening an approved email later with no new reason.

06

Your tools and logins.

Our experts work inside your own Tern, TravelJoy or Travefy and your email. Your host login is never needed.

What works well

  • You add us as a user in your tool.
  • Passwords go through a password manager.
  • You remove our access when the work ends.

What slows us down

  • Sharing one login between people.
  • Sending a password by email or text.

07

Timing.

A setup averages 3 weeks for one kind of trip. Bigger systems take longer. Your dates come in writing before we start.

What works well

  • Your sample trips, templates and notes arrive at the start.
  • Questions get answered while the work is fresh.

What slows us down

  • Waiting on an approval. Every later date moves by the same amount.

08

New work.

Each package covers what's in your written plan. Inside that plan, the work is tweaked until it's right for you.

What works well

  • A new idea is named as new work and priced before it starts.
  • One kind of trip at a time, done well.

What slows us down

  • Adding a second kind of trip halfway through, with no new plan.

09

Your clients, your business.

Eliava never sells travel. Your clients, suppliers and commissions stay yours.

  • Client emails go out under your name, with your OK.
  • We never contact a supplier or traveler as the seller.
  • Traveler details are used only for your work.
  • Eliava signs your NDA and carries E&O insurance.
