FAQ

Do I need the audit?

No. It's optional, and it helps most if you're starting fresh or don't know what's broken. If your systems already work, we skip it.

Can I start small?

Yes. Start with a working session, $95 for 60 minutes, or a Single Fix for $225. Or try Keep it running for one month, with no commitment.

Do you work in my tools?

Yes. I work inside what you already use, like Tern, TravelJoy, Travefy, Trello or even a spreadsheet. If you have no system yet, I'll help you pick one.

How do you get into my tools?

Where your tools allow it, you add me as a user and can remove me anytime. If not, you share a login privately, and I delete it when we're done.

Who talks to my clients?

You do. I never contact your clients on my own, market to them or book them. Anything that goes to a client goes out in your name, after you okay it.

What does the trial cost?

Month 1 is $1,100 for Steady or $2,200 for Embedded, with no commitment. Pick the 3-month start by day 25, and the extra ($110 or $220) comes off month 2. That makes all 3 months $990 or $1,980 each.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Month-to-month plans can stop at the end of any month. If you choose the 3-month start, you agree to those first 3 months.

Are you US-based?

Yes. I'm based in Georgia.

Can you train my overseas assistant?

Yes. I write plain-English steps they can follow, then train them live on your trips. Assistant training starts at $495 for one kind of trip.

Can you help us use AI?

Yes. I set up Claude with your voice, your steps and your templates. Then I teach you and your assistant to use it. I never put card or passport numbers into AI. It's priced after your free Scoping Call.

What happens on the Scoping Call?

We talk through what's piling up and what you need. It's free and takes 25 minutes on Zoom. After the call, you get the plan and price in writing, and you decide from there.

My business is complex. Can you still help?

Yes. We plan it together on a Scoping Call, and you get a written quote first.
