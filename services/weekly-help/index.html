Your questions, answered.

What if I want to stop?

Month 1 has no commitment. You choose what's next around day 25. Month-to-month plans can stop at the end of any month.

How does the quarter plan work?

Pick it by day 25 and the extra $110 you paid comes off month 2. Month 1 counts as the first month of your quarter.

What if I have a slow month?

On the quarter plan, we do more when it's busy and less when it's slow. Your bill stays the same each month.

My commissions come in months later. How does billing work?

You pay the same amount each month, never one big bill up front. The cost stays steady while your commissions catch up.

I have a team. Is there a bigger plan?

Yes. Embedded is for a busier business or an advisor with a team. You get everything here, plus a weekly video recap and one new build each quarter. It's $1,980 a month on the quarter plan, or $2,200 month to month. We take one Embedded client at a time.

Will you talk to my clients?

Only in your name, and only after your OK.
