01 / Why

The work moves. The checking stays.

Most advisors are not short on tools or help. The VA finishes the task, and you check it. AI drafts the email, and you rewrite it. The CRM sends the reminder, and you make sure it went.

  1. Trip lifecycle. Where a trip really begins and ends.
  2. What you remember. The rules that live in your head.
  3. What done means. Completed is not the same as correct.
  4. Where you are needed. Not everything needs your review.
  5. Who owns what. Today's owner and the right one.

Part 2 / Rules that let work leave your desk

  1. Timing rules. Trigger, action, owner, deadline.
  2. Escalation rules. Instead of "ask me if you are unsure."
  3. Tools and AI. Use what you already pay for.
  4. What gets checked. Stop reviewing everything the same way.
  5. Stop, fix, protect. Three changes for the next 30 days.

05 / Questions

Before you start.

Is it really free?

Yes. The skill is free. You need a Claude account to use it.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Setup takes a few minutes and the steps are written out. After that, you answer questions and Claude does the writing.

Does it work with Tern, TravelJoy, or Travefy?

Yes, and with any other setup, even a spreadsheet. It asks what your tools do today. It never logs into them.

What happens to my answers?

The skill runs inside your own Claude account. Nothing is sent to me. Leave out client names, passport numbers, and card details. Describe the work, not the people.

What if I do not have the Skills option in Claude?

The download page also has a one-file version. Attach it to any Claude chat or Project and it works the same way.

Why do you ask for my email?

So I can send you the link and let you know when the skill gets better. You will also get an occasional note on running a travel back office. Unsubscribe anytime.
